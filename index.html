    How Marswalk works

    ▦

    Mars, in squares

    The map is cut into 100 m squares. Each one is checked for steep slopes and loose sand, and for rocks worth studying, using the kind of data NASA's orbiters collect.

    ↝

    Four routes

    Like a GPS app, Marswalk finds the best path between your places, four ways: quickest, safest, most science, and balanced.

    ⌂

    Always get home

    The golden rule: at every step, the astronaut must be able to walk back to base with air to spare. Routes that break it turn red.

    This demo runs on a model landscape shaped like Jezero Crater, built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It is not real mission data.
